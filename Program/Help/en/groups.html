Groups and favorites

A group is an arbitrary named label you can attach to any books across the connected collections, so that you can later find them all together regardless of author, series or even collection. Groups are managed on the Groups tab.

The two built-in groups

"Favorites" and "To read" always exist in the program and cannot be deleted — they are created automatically along with the system database itself. "Favorites" also has an add command of its own ("Add to Favorites", Ctrl+F in the context menu); all the other groups, "To read" among them, are equal to one another and are filled through the "Add to group" submenu.

Creating, renaming, clearing, deleting

Button / commandWhat it does
CreateStarts a new empty group under the name you enter.
Rename groupChanges the name of the selected group without touching its contents.
Clear / "Clear group"Removes every book from the group, leaving the group itself in place but empty.
Delete / "Delete group"Deletes the group together with the list of books in it. The book itself does not disappear from the collection — only its membership of this group does.

"Favorites" and "To read" are protected from renaming and deletion — those two actions are unavailable for them. Clearing, on the other hand, works on them just as it does on any other group.

Adding and removing books

A book is added to a group with the commands "Add to Favorites" (for Favorites only) and "Add to group" (a submenu listing every other existing group), from the Book menu or from the context menu of the book list. To take a book out of the group you are currently viewing, use "Remove from group" — it only appears while you are looking at the contents of a group or of Favorites. The full list of these items is in Book menu and Context menus.

You cannot delete a book from a collection while viewing it through a group tab or Favorites — to delete it you first have to switch to the tab of the collection the book belongs to; see Deleting books. Deleting a book from a collection, on the other hand, removes it at once from every group it belonged to.

Where groups are stored

Unlike ratings and reading progress, which MyHomeLib keeps in the collection's own database, groups are data of the system database (user.dbs2, shared by all collections; see Key terms). Groups therefore do not depend on which collection is currently open, and they are not lost if a collection is disconnected and connected again: a book's BookID is merely a counter, reassigned from scratch when the collection is fully reimported, so a group pointing straight at it would go stale — instead MyHomeLib matches group membership by the book's stable internal identifier (LibID) and restores the link even after a reimport.
