Browsing: authors, series, genres

The Authors, Series and Genres tabs give you three different cross-sections of one and the same collection: the same tree of books, built by author, by series or by genre.

What the three tabs have in common

On the left of each of the three tabs is a tree, and above it a Search box with a button that clears it: the text you type immediately narrows the tree to the nodes that match it. On the right is the list of books of the selected node, and above that a title strip: on the left a caption with the name of the selected node (the author's full name, the series title or the genre name), on the right the Lang combo box and a count of the books. The Lang list does not narrow the tree — it narrows the books of the author, series or genre already selected, the ones shown on the right.

Until you select something in the tree, the caption above the book list is just an ellipsis ("..."); it fills in with the author's full name, the series title or the genre name as soon as you pick a node.

What the tree shows

  • Authors — a flat list of the authors present in the collection.
  • Series — a list of series titles.
  • Genres — a tree of genres and subgenres from the standard genre list (see Genre lists).

Selecting a node in the tree loads into the book list on the right only the books of that author, that series or that genre, and immediately updates the caption and the book count above the list. The book list itself can be shown as a tree (books grouped under an author or series heading) or as a flat table — see Toolbar and View menu.

Alphabet strips

The Cyrillic alphabet and Latin alphabet buttons on the toolbar show or hide a strip of letters of that alphabet above the tree — clicking a letter scrolls the tree to the first node starting with it. The strips are available on the Authors and Series tabs.

Expanding and collapsing the tree

The Expand/Collapse list button on the toolbar fully expands or collapses the tree of books on the right, when that list is shown in tree mode.

The actions available on a right-click in the tree or in the book list are described in Context menus; keyboard shortcuts are in Keyboard shortcuts.
