About MyHomeLib

What this program is

MyHomeLib is a program for managing a home library of e-books. It lets you catalog your own collection of book files, and it also works as a client for libraries running the Librusec engine — Flibusta and similar sites: it searches for books, browses them by author, series or genre, and downloads them straight into a collection.
