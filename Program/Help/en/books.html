Working with books

This section covers everything you can do with the books of a collection that is already connected: how they get there, and how to read, mark, search, group, edit, send to a device and delete them. The mechanics of connecting and updating collections are described in Working with collections; the menus and context menus through which most of these actions are carried out are in Book menu and Context menus.
